# PinTube name and logo

The PinTube **source code** is free software under the [AGPL-3.0](LICENSE). The **name "PinTube" and the pin logo** are not covered by that license.

You may:

- use, study, modify and share the code under the AGPL-3.0;
- say that your project is "based on PinTube" or "a fork of PinTube".

You may not, without written permission:

- publish a modified version (in an extension store, on a website, or as a service) under the name PinTube or with the PinTube logo;
- use the name or logo in a way that suggests your version is the official one or is endorsed by the PinTube project.

If you publish a fork, please give it a different name and icon. This protects users from confusing an unofficial build with the official one.
